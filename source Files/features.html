06

Random Level Generator

⚡ PROCEDURAL COMBAT GENERATOR

Normal levels are generated through the game's dedicated level-generation system. The generator does not only determine one aspect of the encounter. Normal levels generate formation data, enemy type, movement behavior and firing behavior. This creates combinations of gameplay systems rather than relying on a single permanently fixed enemy wave.

FORMATION Rectangle, Triangle, Diamond, Circle and Random formation options.
ENEMY TYPE Basic, Speedy, Tank, Destroyer and later mixed enemy configurations.
MOVEMENT Straight, Left/Right, Dive and Zigzag movement behavior.
FIRE TYPE Single, Diagonal and Triple firing behavior.
DIFFICULTY Enemy speed, health, cooldown, bullet speed and attack pressure scale through progression.
ELITE SYSTEM Elite presence increases during later stages of the campaign.

ACT V

ENDGAME

08

Level 31+ — Warzone

⚠ THE GAME STARTS GETTING WILD

Level 31 begins Act IV: WARZONE. This is where mixed enemy encounters are introduced. Instead of relying on one enemy type, different enemy classes can appear together. As progression continues, enemy health, movement, firing pressure and other difficulty values continue scaling. From Level 40 onward, non-boss levels can also receive reinforcements. When the remaining enemy count becomes low, surviving enemies can be promoted to Elite, making the final portion of a wave especially dangerous.
